# UniHelp - University Helpdesk Chatbot Documentation

## Overview
UniHelp is the official automated helpdesk of the university, designed to assist with admissions, enrollment, course registration, and fee-related matters. It provides short, precise, and policy-aligned responses in a formal administrative tone.

## How to Use
Access the chatbot through the university website or student portal and ask questions related to university administrative processes.

## Supported Query Types and Example Responses

### 1. Fee Structure Queries
**Questions to ask:**
- What is the fee structure?
- How much are the tuition fees?
- What are the total fees for a semester?
- Breakdown of fees

**Example Response:**
```
Here's a breakdown of the fee structure for Software Engineering students:

• Tuition Fees: $1200 per semester
• Examination Fees: $200 per semester
• Hostel Fees: $300 per month (optional)
• Transport Fees: $100 per month (optional)

For the most accurate and up-to-date details, please check the university portal or contact the accounts office.
```

### 2. Payment Methods Queries
**Questions to ask:**
- How do I pay fees?
- What are the payment methods?
- Can I pay online?
- How to make fee payment?

**Example Response:**
```
You can pay your fees through the following methods:

• Online Payment:
  - University Portal: Log in and use UPI, net banking, or credit/debit card
  - Payment Gateway: Secure transactions with instant confirmation

• Offline Payment:
  - Bank Challan: Download from portal, pay at bank, upload receipt

Step-by-step guidance:
1. Log in to the student portal
2. Navigate to 'Fee Payment' section
3. Select payment method and amount
4. Complete the transaction
5. Save the receipt for records

For any issues, contact the accounts office.
```

### 3. Fee Deadlines Queries
**Questions to ask:**
- When is the fee deadline?
- What is the last date for payment?
- When do fees need to be paid?

**Example Response:**
```
Important fee deadlines:

• Semester Fee Payment: Due by April 5
• Late Payment Penalty: Starts from April 6
• Refund Requests: Within first two weeks of semester

Missing deadlines may result in:
• Late fee penalties ($50 per week)
• Temporary deactivation of course registration
• Possible enrollment cancellation if delayed further

Please pay promptly to avoid penalties.
```

### 4. Late Fees and Penalties Queries
**Questions to ask:**
- What happens if I miss the deadline?
- How much is the late fee?
- What are the penalties for late payment?

**Example Response:**
```
Late fee penalties:

• Penalty Amount: $50 per week after the deadline
• Maximum Penalty: Up to 10% of total fees
• Consequences:
  - Course registration blocked
  - Exam ineligibility
  - Enrollment suspension

To avoid penalties:
• Pay fees before April 5
• Contact accounts office for extensions if needed
• Monitor payment status on the portal

For assistance with late payments, reach out to the accounts office.
```

### 5. Refund Queries
**Questions to ask:**
- How do I get a refund?
- What is the refund policy?
- Can I get my money back?

**Example Response:**
```
Fee refund policy:

• Eligibility: Refunds allowed within first two weeks of semester
• Refund Amount: Full refund minus processing fees ($50)
• Processing Time: 7-14 business days
• Conditions:
  - Withdrawal request submitted
  - No outstanding dues
  - Documents returned (ID card, etc.)

To request a refund:
1. Submit withdrawal form
2. Pay any outstanding fees
3. Contact accounts office for processing

For detailed policy, check the university website.
```

### 6. Enrollment Status Queries
**Questions to ask:**
- Am I enrolled?
- What is my enrollment status?
- How do I check if I'm enrolled?
- I paid fees but not enrolled yet

**Example Response:**
```
Enrollment confirmation process:

Enrollment is confirmed after:
• Successful fee payment
• Document verification (ID, certificates)
• Academic office approval

Status Check:
• Log in to student portal
• Check 'Enrollment Status' section
• Contact admissions if delayed beyond 48 hours

If payment is successful but enrollment pending:
• Allow 24-48 hours for processing
• Ensure all documents are uploaded
• Verify email for confirmation notifications

For urgent issues, contact the admissions office.
```

### 7. Course Registration Queries
**Questions to ask:**
- How do I register for courses?
- What is course registration?
- How to select subjects?

**Example Response:**
```
Course registration process:

Prerequisites:
• Confirmed enrollment status
• Fees paid in full
• No outstanding penalties

Steps to Register:
1. Log in to student portal
2. Go to 'Course Registration'
3. Select semester and courses
4. Submit for approval
5. Pay any additional course fees if required

Timelines:
• Registration opens: April 10
• Deadline: April 20
• Late registration: With penalty

For course selection advice, consult your academic advisor.
```

### 8. Admission Confirmation Queries
**Questions to ask:**
- How do I confirm admission?
- What are the admission steps?
- I got admission, what next?

**Example Response:**
```
Admission confirmation steps:

After admission offer:
1. Accept the offer online
2. Pay admission fees ($200 non-refundable)
3. Submit required documents:
   - ID proof
   - Academic certificates
   - Medical certificate
   - Passport-sized photos
4. Complete enrollment process
5. Receive student ID and portal access

Confirmation Timeline:
• Document verification: 3-5 days
• Enrollment processing: 1-2 weeks
• Full access: After fee payment

If you haven't received confirmation, check your email or contact admissions.
```

### 9. Installments and Scholarships Queries
**Questions to ask:**
- Are there installments available?
- What scholarships are there?
- How to apply for financial aid?

**Example Response:**
```
Installment and scholarship options:

Installments:
• Available for tuition fees only
• 2-3 installments per semester
• Interest-free for eligible students
• Application required before semester start

Scholarships:
• Merit-based: For GPA above 3.5
• Need-based: For financial hardship
• Sports/Arts: For extracurricular achievements
• Amount: Up to 50% of tuition fees

To apply:
• Submit scholarship application
• Provide supporting documents
• Deadlines: Before fee payment

For eligibility check, contact the financial aid office.
```

- Hello
- What is the weather?
- Unrelated questions

**Example Response:**
"This service handles only admissions, enrollment, course registration, and fee-related queries."
